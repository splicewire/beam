The agreement prompt a refused page visit lands on (agreements 07): `beamAgreementsPages` adds the Inertia page
`agreements/required`, which a host merges into its `pages`. It asks with 06's step-up dialog. Accepting records the
Agreement and goes where the person was going. Declining stays on the prompt with a way back and "Review again". A
version that changed on the way is never shown: the page offers to try again instead.

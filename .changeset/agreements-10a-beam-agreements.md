New package: agreements and consents in the browser. Its first screens are the account's "Agreements & consents" and
the organisation owner's view (`AgreementsAndConsents`, `OrganisationAgreements`). Each lists an entries resource
through frame's shells and opens an entry read-only, with the resource's declared withdraw action. The host mounts its
frame kit and passes the resource key.
